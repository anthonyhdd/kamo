KAMO 🦎

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

KAMO is a camera photo-toy that lets you place a small 3D figure into your photos, paint it to blend in, and share a "spot the figure" challenge. We built KAMO to collect as little data as possible.

The short version

We do not have accounts, and we never sell your data. Everything you create is made on your device. One thing does leave it: if you send someone a challenge, the finished image is uploaded so they can play it in their browser — see Challenges. Nothing is uploaded unless you open the share sheet to send it.

Camera & photos

KAMO uses your camera and photo library only to let you create and save your images. Your camera feed, the photos you pick, and the painting itself are processed entirely on your device. We never receive your camera feed or your photo library. Saving an image writes it to your own photo library, with your permission.

The one exception is a challenge you choose to send — described next.

Challenges

A challenge is a link that lets someone else try to find the figure you hid, in their browser, without installing anything. Making that possible means the finished image has to leave your device.

When. Only when you open the share sheet to send a hide. Finishing a round, painting, or saving to your photo library uploads nothing.

What. The finished image — your photo with the figure painted into it — plus the figure's position (needed to tell a correct guess from a wrong one), how long your round took, and how much of the figure you covered. No name, no email, no account: we have none.

Where. On our hosting provider, Supabase, in the United States. The image is reachable by anyone who has the link. The link contains a random 16-character identifier, is not listed anywhere, and is not indexed by search engines — but it is not password-protected, so treat sending a challenge the same way you would treat sending the photo itself.

For how long. Challenges are deleted automatically 30 days after they are created, image included. There is no way to keep one longer.

Reporting. Every challenge page has a Report link. A reported challenge is taken down automatically once a few people have flagged it, and you can also write to us at the address below to have any challenge removed. Tell us the link and we will delete it.

Purchases

KAMO+ is available as a one-time purchase (Lifetime) and, where offered, as an auto-renewable weekly subscription. Both are handled by Apple's App Store and our payments provider, RevenueCat, to unlock and restore your purchase. We receive an anonymous purchase status only (whether Pro is active). We never see your payment details — those are handled by Apple. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's.

Analytics & tracking

KAMO uses a small set of third-party services to understand how the app is used and whether our ads work:

Usage analytics. We use Amplitude to collect anonymous usage events (e.g. which screens are opened, whether a creation is completed). This helps us improve the app.

Ad measurement & attribution. We use AppsFlyer and the TikTok and Meta measurement SDKs to understand which ads bring new players — for example, whether an install came from a TikTok or Instagram ad. This may involve your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow it: iOS asks for your permission first (App Tracking Transparency), and saying no doesn't limit the app in any way.

Crash reporting. We use Sentry to receive anonymous crash and error reports so we can fix what breaks. These contain technical diagnostics only.

We do not sell your data. None of the services above receive your photos or creations — the only provider that stores an image is Supabase, and only for a challenge you chose to send (see Challenges). You can change your tracking choice anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the image you created is handed to the app you choose (e.g. Instagram or Snapchat) through the standard iOS sharing tools. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

KAMO is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

We don't keep accounts, so there is no profile to access or delete. Analytics and attribution data is keyed to random or device identifiers, not your name or email. Deleting the app removes locally-stored settings; you can also reset your device's advertising identifier in iOS Settings. A challenge you sent expires by itself within 30 days, and we will delete one sooner on request — send us the link. For any other data request, email us at the address below.
