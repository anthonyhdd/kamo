KAMO 🦎

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

KAMO is a camera photo-toy that lets you place a small 3D figure into your photos, paint it to blend in, and share a "spot the figure" challenge. We built KAMO to collect as little data as possible.

The short version

We do not have accounts. We do not upload, store, or sell your photos. Everything you create is processed on your device. We collect limited usage and device data for analytics and to measure our advertising (details below) — we never sell it.

Camera & photos

KAMO uses your camera and photo library only to let you create and save your images. Your camera feed and any photo you pick are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to our servers (we don't have any). Saving an image writes it to your own photo library, with your permission.

Purchases

KAMO Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple's App Store and our payments provider, RevenueCat, to unlock and restore your purchase. We receive an anonymous purchase status only (whether Pro is active). We never see your payment details — those are handled by Apple. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's.

Analytics & tracking

KAMO uses a small set of third-party services to understand how the app is used and whether our ads work:

Usage analytics. We use Amplitude to collect anonymous usage events (e.g. which screens are opened, whether a creation is completed). This helps us improve the app.

Ad measurement & attribution. We use AppsFlyer and the TikTok measurement SDK to understand which ads bring new players — for example, whether an install came from a TikTok or Instagram ad. This may involve your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow it: iOS asks for your permission first (App Tracking Transparency), and saying no doesn't limit the app in any way.

We do not sell your data, and none of these services receive your photos or creations. You can change your tracking choice anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the image you created is handed to the app you choose (e.g. Instagram or Snapchat) through the standard iOS sharing tools. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

KAMO is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

We don't keep accounts, so there is no profile to access or delete. Analytics and attribution data is keyed to random or device identifiers, not your name or email. Deleting the app removes locally-stored settings; you can also reset your device's advertising identifier in iOS Settings. For any data request, email us at the address below.
